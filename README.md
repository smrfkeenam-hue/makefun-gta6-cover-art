**Vice Press** — an offline web app that turns any photo and caption into GTA 6-style cover art: the multi-panel poster look with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon grade, exported as a PNG to share. Photos never leave the machine; everything runs in the browser from static files.

What it does:

- **7 layouts** — Hero + Three, Four Up, Nine Panel (title block in the middle), Full Bleed, Tilted Snapshots, Split Duo, Film Strip
- **6 colour grades** — Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Gold, Mono Noir, Heat Haze, Ocean Drive, each with its own title gradient
- **Any photo** — upload your own or use the six built-in samples; click a panel to select it, drag its photo to move it, scroll or slide to zoom, per panel
- **Words** — title in Anton with a sunset-gradient fill and white keyline, script subtitle in Pacifico, starburst badge, 5-star heat meter
- **Finish** — film grain, vignette, white frame, halftone title bands
- **Fast** — one click exports the cover, or all four post sizes at once: 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080, 1080×1920 (Story)
- **Undo / redo / shuffle / surprise me** — full history, one-click photo shuffle, random layout + grade + title

Fonts are Anton, Pacifico and Bebas Neue under the SIL Open Font License (see `app/fonts/OFL.txt`). Sample photos were made with an image model; no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
